From The Journal

A few real notes, kept simple.

No long articles. Just short entries from days that left something behind.

7 May | Lower Austria | Roe deer

First light reached the grass before the woods.

The morning stayed cold and very still. Before the sun had fully reached the edge of the field, two roe deer were already moving across the open ground. Nothing dramatic happened, but it felt like the kind of morning that reminds you why getting up early is worth it.

18 June | Local pond | Evening water

The whole water looked asleep until the last minutes.

Almost no wind, hardly any movement and long quiet stretches where nothing seemed to change. Then right before dark, the surface came alive for a moment and turned the whole evening into something worth remembering on the drive home.

Late September | Woodland edge | Red deer

The woods felt different before anything was seen.

That was the strongest part of the evening. Not the sight itself, but the feeling that the whole ground had shifted. Sound carried farther, every step mattered more and the air had the weight that only comes at that time of year.
