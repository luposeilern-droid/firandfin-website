Our Story

Hello, I am Leopold Seilern, a young hunter and fisherman, and this passion has been with me since I was very young.

Some of my earliest memories are sitting with my father on the high seat at around six years old and catching my first trout in a small stream.

Fur & Fin is my place to share that world and to show that hunting and fishing are about patience, responsibility, respect and time in nature, not only about the catch or the shot.
