# Useful Next App Features

- `Saved seasons`: mark favorite species and countries for faster checking
- `Weather`: quick hunting and fishing weather on the home page
- `Sunrise / sunset`: very useful for planning morning and evening outings
- `Moon phase`: useful especially for wild boar and night feeling
- `Field log`: save your own short hunt and fishing notes inside the app
- `Map spots`: store favorite stands, ponds or fishing places privately
- `Gear checklist`: quick packing list before a hunt or fishing trip
- `Push reminders`: reminders for open seasons, trips or gear to bring
- `Photo gallery`: your own catches, animals and field moments in one place
- `Offline season guide`: season data still visible without internet
